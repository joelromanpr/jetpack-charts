## From the first point to a live market

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Built for changing data" icon="bars">
    Bounded rolling buffers, immutable snapshots, and visible-range rendering keep growing feeds manageable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trading is a first-class use case" icon="rocket">
    Candles, volume, depth, and six indicators. Link the viewport and crosshair across price and indicator panels.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fits your app" icon="pencil">
